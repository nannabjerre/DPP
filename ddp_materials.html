HDPE

High-density polyethylene (HDPE) is a thermoplastic polymer produced from the monomer ethylene. It is sometimes called "alkathene" or "polythene" when used for HDPE pipes. It is often used in the production of plastic bottles, corrosion-resistant piping, and plastic lumber.
